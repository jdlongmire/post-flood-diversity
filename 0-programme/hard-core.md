# Hard Core

The hard core defines the programme. It is not offered for ordinary refutation.
Adjust the protective belt to meet difficulties; change the core only by an
explicit change of commitment, recorded as a programme revision.

## The five commitments

Preserved essentially unchanged from the seed specification:

1. **A global Flood catastrophe occurred.** Consumed as an upstream historical
   commitment from the Flood geology line
   (`jdlongmire/global-flood-hydrotectonic-model`). PDP does not re-argue the
   geological mechanism.
2. **It bottlenecked air-breathing land animals and birds to a preserved
   breeding stock.** The origin set is small and simultaneous.
3. **The preserved stock was genetically sound.** Not a degraded remnant. The
   stock is treated as intact and heterozygous, not as an impoverished survivor
   population.
4. **The stock possessed substantial latent biological potential within its
   respective body plans.** Later within-plan variety is largely an unpacking
   of what the founders carried, plus the auxiliary processes of the belt.
5. **The preserved units were kinds rather than subsequent Linnaean species.**
   Kinds are the unit of the preserved stock; the species lists of later
   catalogues are unpacked plans, not a second creation.

## What the core does not contain

- **Kind width.** Whether a kind maps to a body plan, a family, or some other
  cluster is a protective-belt choice (`1-boundary/kind-boundary.md`). The
  core requires only that the number of preserved units equal the number of
  plans treated as primitive. Keep body-plan-level kinds out of the core;
  the distinction will matter enormously later.
- **The genetic route.** Standing variation, recombination, regulatory
  change, selection, isolation, chromosomal rearrangement, and mutation are
  belt auxiliaries (`2-theory/`), adjustable without touching the core.
- **Ark logistics.** Hull dimensions, feed, water, and waste are boundary
  conditions (`1-boundary/ark-boundary.md`, `2-theory/ark-interface/`),
  not core commitments.
- **Chronology details.** The unpacking window (about 5,500 years to the
  mid-1700s catalogues) dates the belt; it does not create the species
  (`1-boundary/chronology.md`).

## Standing rule

Resist expanding this hard core. Every item added to the core is an item the
programme can no longer adjust when the evidence presses. The seed
specification's five commitments are sufficient; the belt has room for
everything else.
