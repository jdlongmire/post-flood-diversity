# Epistemic Status

**Current status: PDP-0. Boundary specification, not a tested explanation.**

## What is established

- The hard core is stated and stable (`hard-core.md`).
- The boundary conditions are stated: chronology, Ark hull, maturity,
  kind-width options (`1-boundary/`).
- The protective belt is inventoried: kind width, genetic route, hull
  logistics (`registry/AUXILIARIES.md`).
- The observations are fenced: what they may and may not do
  (`registry/OBSERVATIONS.md`, charter Rule 1).

## What is granted, not demonstrated

Two grants do the real work (seed specification, section 6):

1. **Historical:** the Flood happened, and this was the stock.
2. **Biological:** each origin population already carried enough heterozygous,
   regulable variation that the later breeds and close species of its plan
   fall inside the auxiliary routes.

The allele cap no longer forbids the second grant. The watched cases do not
demonstrate it for a whole plan's species list from one pair. That missing
analysis is the current explanatory deficit (`registry/OPEN-PROBLEMS.md`,
OP-01).

## The framework is consistent

Flood makes the origin set small and simultaneous. The Ark carries intact,
heterozygous body-plan stocks, young and small enough for the hull. Catalogues
a few thousand years later are unpacked plans, not a second creation. Rate
examples show that within-plan spread from a variable stock can be rapid.
They are not evidence the Flood occurred.

## Maturity ladder and promotion gates

- **PDP-0 Boundary specification.** Current. Gate to PDP-1: charter, hard
  core, heuristics, auxiliary register, terminology, and traceability in
  place and internally consistent.
- **PDP-1 Formal research programme.** Gate to PDP-2: streams PDP-01..06
  staffed with work packages; kind registry seeded with competing
  hypotheses.
- **PDP-2 Quantified feasibility model.** Gate to PDP-3: PDP-03 delivers a
  quantified founder-genome feasibility model under the four-copy constraint.
- **PDP-3 Successful worked kinds.** Gate to PDP-4: at least one taxon
  carried end to end (founder stock to observed descendants) under PDP-06.
- **PDP-4 Cross-kind explanatory model.** Gate to PDP-5: worked kinds across
  the difficulty gradient, including a severe test (Chiroptera).
- **PDP-5 Differential predictions.** Gate to PDP-6: predictions that
  discriminate PDP from rival accounts, registered in advance.
- **PDP-6 Empirically tested programme.** Severe tests run; appraisal
  published (`5-predictions/appraisal.md`).

The ladder prevents "internally possible" from quietly becoming
"demonstrated."
