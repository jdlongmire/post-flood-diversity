# Scope

## In scope

- Whether a small preserved post-Flood breeding stock can account for observed
  terrestrial biological diversity within the programme's chronology, genetic
  constraints, ecological processes, and Ark boundary conditions.
- Kind boundary determination (morphology, reproductive compatibility,
  comparative genomics, developmental architecture, phylogenetic structure).
- Founder genome feasibility under the four-copy autosomal constraint.
- Diversification dynamics: recombination, selection, regulatory change,
  isolation, chromosomal rearrangement, mutation, speciation.
- Post-Flood ecology: empty niches, dispersal, biogeography.
- The Ark biological/logistics interface: population size, maturity,
  survivability, feed, water, waste. Interfaced with, not conflated with,
  Flood geology.
- Kind-by-kind demonstration, taxa PDP-TAX-001 through PDP-TAX-008.

## Out of scope

- **Geological mechanism.** The Flood's geological causes and effects belong
  to `jdlongmire/global-flood-hydrotectonic-model`. PDP consumes the Flood as
  an upstream historical commitment.
- **Dated durations from fossils or molecular clocks.** Outside the programme
  unless explicitly added as an auxiliary (`1-boundary/chronology.md`).
- **Origin of the body plans themselves.** The programme starts from
  preserved, already-potent stocks within their plans; it does not explain
  how the plans arose.
- **Invertebrate land animals** (arthropod, snail, worm layouts), unless
  explicitly brought under the same rule (`1-boundary/body-plan-candidates.md`).
- **Aquatic animals**, excluded by the text; and insects or other groups
  carried with fodder, unless the core's preserved stock is required for them
  (`2-theory/ark-interface/`).

## Boundary with the evidence

`3-evidence/` records observed diversification rates and empirical bounds.
Extrapolating those mechanisms to an Ark founder population is a model claim
and belongs in `4-models/`. The two are never merged silently.
