# Terminology

Canonical terms for the programme. Use these consistently; do not mint
synonyms without registering them here.

- **Kind.** The unit of the preserved stock. Not a Linnaean species. Kind
  width (body plan, family, other) is a belt choice, not a core commitment.
- **Body plan.** A coarse morphological layout (e.g., mammal, bird, lizard).
  Candidate list: `1-boundary/body-plan-candidates.md`. Finer clusters
  (carnivoran jaw, hoof, rodent gnawing set, bat wing) are modifications of
  one plan unless the belt redraws the kind boundary.
- **Preserved stock.** The breeding stock bottlenecked by the Flood: male and
  female per kind (not necessarily full-grown), genetically sound, carrying
  substantial latent potential within its body plan.
- **Founder pair / founder population.** The embarked representatives of a
  kind. Standing constraint: at any one autosomal locus, a pair holds at
  most four copies.
- **Allele cap (four-copy constraint).** Descendants have only the alleles
  the founders carried, plus mutations that arise and spread later.
  Recombination does not invent a fifth allele at a locus. Changing this
  changes the origin stock (charter Rule 2).
- **Unpacking.** Diversification of a plan's later breeds and close species
  from the founder stock, via the belt's genetic route and ecological
  processes.
- **Hard core.** The five commitments (`hard-core.md`). Not offered for
  ordinary refutation.
- **Protective belt.** Adjustable auxiliaries: kind width, genetic route,
  hull logistics (`registry/AUXILIARIES.md`).
- **Boundary specification.** What PDP-0 is: the frame is consistent, but the
  whole-plan biological grant has not been demonstrated by the observed
  examples.
- **Grant.** A load-bearing assumption doing explanatory work but not yet
  demonstrated (the historical grant; the biological grant). Tracked in
  `registry/OPEN-PROBLEMS.md`.
- **Rate example.** An observed case of rapid diversification (dogs, finches,
  Madeira mice, livestock). Usable to establish biological possibility or
  empirical rate bounds. Not a demonstration of the post-Flood history
  (charter Rule 1).
- **Kind-by-kind demonstration.** The PDP-06 work product: this pair, these
  alleles and pathways, this much selection, these descendants by the
  catalogues.
- **Programme revision.** A change to the founder population, chronology, or
  hard-core historical event. Classified and recorded as a revision, never
  absorbed silently as a belt adjustment (charter Rule 2).
