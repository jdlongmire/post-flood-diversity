# PDP-TAX-001: Canidae Kind-Boundary Dossier

**Stream:** PDP-02
**Status:** first-pass evidence dossier
**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Role:** calibration case. This dossier constrains candidate boundaries; it does not establish a historical Ark kind.

## Candidate hypotheses

| ID | Candidate partition | First-pass classification | Principal burden |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAN-H-BP | Mammalian body-plan founder includes Canidae | STRAINED | Must explain mammal-wide developmental, genomic, reproductive, and ecological disparity downstream |
| CAN-H-O | Carnivora-level founder includes Canidae | STRAINED | Must explain major carnivoran family-level architectures and genomic discontinuities |
| CAN-H-F | Canidae-level founder | COMPATIBLE | Must quantify family-wide founder genomic capacity and rapid radiation |
| CAN-H-C | Canis/wolf-like narrower cluster | COMPATIBLE for that cluster, INCOMPLETE for family | Explains documented reticulation inside Canis but requires additional founder units for other canid lineages |

Classification means compatibility with current PDP constraints, not historical demonstration.

## 1. Morphology

Canidae contains substantial morphological and ecological spread. The South American radiation includes, among other forms, the short-legged bush dog and long-legged maned wolf. Whole-genome work treats these disparate forms within a coherent canid radiation.

**Implication:** family-level continuity is not excluded by gross morphology. Morphological range itself does not demonstrate descent from a single post-Flood pair.

**Contrary pressure on broad hypotheses:** moving the boundary to Carnivora or Mammalia greatly increases the number and magnitude of derived architectures that PDP-04 must explain. This dossier does not yet establish a mechanism sufficient for that burden.

## 2. Reproductive compatibility and gene flow

Genome-scale studies report extensive gene flow among crown-group Canis lineages. Reported signals include gray wolf/coyote admixture, gray-wolf and Ethiopian-wolf contributions to African golden wolf ancestry, and gene flow involving golden jackal and other canids. Wolf-dog admixture is independently documented across multiple populations.

**Implication:** reproductive isolation among named Canis species is porous. Named species boundaries therefore cannot be equated mechanically with PDP kind boundaries.

**Limit:** evidence of gene flow among Canis does not demonstrate reproductive continuity across the entire family Canidae.

## 3. Comparative genomics

Whole-genome phylogenomic studies recover structured relationships across Canidae and document reticulation rather than a strictly bifurcating history in several lineages. A whole-genome study of all extant South American canid species reported a single ancestral radiation for that sampled clade and extensive historical gene flow among recently separated lineages.

Canid karyotype evolution is unusually rearranged relative to many other carnivorans. Comparative chromosome work reports extensive disruption/rearrangement of conserved mammalian regions in canine karyotypes; later comparative work also identifies elevated chromosome-number evolution in Canidae.

**Implication:** chromosome rearrangement is empirically available as a within-canid process and cannot automatically be treated as evidence for separate kinds.

**PDP constraint:** conventional inferred divergence dates reported by these studies are recorded as authors' model outputs only. They are not imported as PDP chronology.

## 4. Developmental architecture

The present evidence set demonstrates large phenotypic range and identifies regulatory loci contributing to morphology. For example, published genomic work identifies a non-coding IGF1-associated variant affecting body size across ancient and modern canids.

This is evidence that substantial phenotypic effects can be mediated through regulatory variation. It is not yet a developmental derivation of the entire family from one founder genome.

**Evidence gap:** PDP-02 still requires a systematic developmental comparison of craniofacial, limb, dentition, body-size, reproductive, and sensory architectures across canid genera.

## 5. Phylogenetic structure

Current genomic phylogenies recover Canidae as a structured clade while also showing reticulation. The tree is therefore neither biologically meaningless nor sufficient by itself to define a PDP kind.

PDP records:
- nested genomic structure as an observation/model result;
- gene-flow edges as evidence of biological continuity where demonstrated;
- inferred conventional divergence times as external model outputs, not PDP elapsed chronology.

## Boundary appraisal

### CAN-H-BP: Mammalian body-plan boundary
**STRAINED.** Nothing in this canid dossier alone logically excludes a mammal-wide founder, but the hypothesis exports an enormous unquantified burden to PDP-03 and PDP-04. Canid evidence cannot discharge that burden.

### CAN-H-O: Carnivora-level boundary
**STRAINED.** Comparative chromosome evidence shows both shared carnivoran homologies and substantial canid-specific rearrangement. No evidence assembled here demonstrates that one post-Flood carnivoran founder can generate all carnivoran families inside the programme chronology.

### CAN-H-F: Canidae-level boundary
**COMPATIBLE.** Family-wide genomic coherence, substantial within-family morphological spread, documented reticulation, and karyotypic remodeling leave a Canidae-level founder hypothesis open. The evidence does not demonstrate a single post-Flood Canidae pair.

### CAN-H-C: Canis/wolf-like cluster
**COMPATIBLE within scope.** This is the strongest direct continuity case because interspecific gene flow is observed genomically across several named taxa. It is too narrow to account for all Canidae and therefore implies multiple canid founder units if adopted as the kind boundary.

## Downstream burden handoff

### PDP-03 Founder Genome Feasibility
For CAN-H-F quantify at minimum:
- founder heterozygosity required to seed extant family-wide polymorphism;
- loci where extant diversity exceeds the four-copy founder cap and therefore requires post-founder mutation;
- structural-variant and karyotype pathways;
- regulatory variation needed for major size and morphology ranges;
- lineage-specific sequence/gene-content burden.

CAN-H-BP and CAN-H-O must be modeled separately. They may not inherit CAN-H-F feasibility.

### PDP-04 Diversification Dynamics
Quantify:
- number of generations available under programme chronology;
- rate of sorting of standing variation;
- mutation supply;
- fixation/segregation under population expansion;
- reproductive isolation and introgression;
- geographic dispersal and niche occupation.

## First-pass conclusion

Canidae supports the methodological decision to keep kind boundaries independent of Linnaean species rank. Genomic evidence of reticulation makes some named species boundaries demonstrably porous. A Canidae-level candidate remains open, while broader Carnivora- and Mammalia-level candidates carry much larger unresolved burdens. No candidate is established as the historical Ark kind by this dossier.
