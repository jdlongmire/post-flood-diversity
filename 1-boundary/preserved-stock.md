# Preserved Stock

## The bottleneck

The Flood bottlenecked air-breathing land animals and birds to a preserved
breeding stock: a male and a female per kind. The origin set is small and
simultaneous.

## Properties of the stock (hard-core commitments 3 and 4)

- **Genetically sound.** Not a degraded remnant. The stock is treated as
  intact and heterozygous, not as an impoverished survivor population.
- **Latent potential within body plans.** The stock already carried
  substantial heterozygous, regulable variation within its respective body
  plans. Later within-plan variety is largely an unpacking of what the
  founders carried, plus the auxiliary processes of the belt.

## What the stock is not

- Not a long, unbottlenecked radiation (negative heuristic).
- Not a genetically impoverished remnant (negative heuristic).
- Not the Linnaean species lists (hull negative heuristic).

## The allele cap

Standing constraint: at any one autosomal locus, a pair holds at most four
copies. Descendants have only those alleles, plus mutations that arise and
spread later. Recombination does not invent a fifth allele at that locus.
Full treatment: `2-theory/genetics/allele-constraints.md`.

An auxiliary that gives the pair more than four alleles at a locus changes
the origin stock. Per charter Rule 2, that is a programme revision, not a
belt adjustment.
