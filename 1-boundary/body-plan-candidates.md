## Finer clusters

Carnivoran jaw, hoof, rodent gnawing set, bat wing: modifications of one
plan, not extra origins, unless the belt redraws the kind boundary on
evidence (`kind-boundary.md`).

## Invertebrate land animals

Arthropod, snail, and worm layouts are a separate short list, included only
if explicitly brought under the same rule. Currently out of scope
(`0-programme/scope.md`).

## Status

Candidates, not conclusions. PDP-02 adjudicates body-plan vs family-level
hypotheses. Registered in `registry/KINDS.md`.
