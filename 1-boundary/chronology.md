# Chronology (boundary)

## The unpacking window

Unpacking runs from about 5,500 years ago to the start of systematic species
catalogues (Linnaeus, mid-1700s). Animals later named are already in place
when first systematically recorded.

## What the chronology does and does not do

- It **dates the belt**: the diversification mechanisms operate inside this
  window.
- It **does not create the species**: the window is a constraint on rate
  models (`2-theory/diversification/rate-models.md`), not an explanation of
  diversity.

## Excluded unless added as an auxiliary

A dated duration taken from fossils or molecular clocks is outside this
programme unless explicitly added as an auxiliary and registered in
`registry/AUXILIARIES.md`.

## Interfaces

- `2-theory/diversification/rate-models.md` - rate bounds must fit the window.
- `registry/OPEN-PROBLEMS.md` - OP-02 (feasibility within the window).
- Charter Rule 2: changing the chronology to resolve a difficulty is a
  programme revision, not a belt adjustment.
