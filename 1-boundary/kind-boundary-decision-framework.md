# Kind Boundary Decision Framework

**Stream:** PDP-02
**Status:** active methodology
**Purpose:** compare candidate preserved-stock boundaries without assuming that a biblical kind maps to any one Linnaean rank.

## 1. Candidate partitions

Every worked taxon is evaluated under the broadest biologically meaningful competing partitions available:

- **H-BP:** coarse body-plan boundary.
- **H-O:** order-level or comparable major clade boundary.
- **H-F:** family-level or comparable clade boundary.
- **H-C:** narrower genomic/reproductive cluster where evidence warrants it.

These are hypotheses, not definitions of kind. A taxon may leave multiple hypotheses live.

## 2. Evidence dimensions

Each candidate boundary receives a dossier in five dimensions.

1. **Morphology:** conserved architecture, discontinuities, and derived structures.
2. **Reproductive compatibility:** direct crosses, hybrid fertility, reproductive barriers, and documented introgression.
3. **Comparative genomics:** shared synteny, sequence divergence, structural variants, gene-family differences, and lineage-specific content.
4. **Developmental architecture:** whether proposed descendant forms can plausibly arise through modification of shared developmental systems without assuming the conclusion.
5. **Phylogenetic structure:** topology and nested grouping are recorded as observations/model outputs. Conventional divergence dates are not imported as PDP chronology unless separately registered as an auxiliary.

## 3. Classification

PDP-02 does not award a winner by aggregate score. Evidence is classified by constraint:

- **COMPATIBLE:** evidence does not presently exclude the candidate boundary.
- **STRAINED:** candidate remains possible only with substantial unresolved explanatory burden.
- **INCOMPATIBLE:** a stated PDP constraint is violated.
- **UNRESOLVED:** evidence is insufficient or materially conflicting.

A boundary can be COMPATIBLE and still carry a high burden. Compatibility is not demonstration.

## 4. Burden fields

For every candidate boundary record:

- descendant diversity to be explained;
- founder-stock size assumed;
- known reproductive discontinuities;
- major morphological/developmental innovations requiring explanation;
- genomic diversity burden passed to PDP-03;
- diversification burden passed to PDP-04;
- geographic/dispersal burden;
- evidence gaps;
- auxiliary assumptions required.

Broad boundaries are therefore allowed to survive PDP-02 while exposing a larger downstream burden.

## 5. Decision rules

1. No Linnaean rank is privileged by definition.
2. Extant hybridization can support continuity but absence of observed hybridization does not alone establish separate kinds.
3. Morphological similarity alone does not establish a kind boundary.
4. Phylogenetic nesting is evidence to be explained, not a synonym for elapsed chronology.
5. A boundary requiring a change to the hard core is not resolved inside PDP-02.
6. PDP-02 must preserve competing boundaries when the evidence underdetermines the partition.
7. Downstream PDP-03 calculations must state exactly which PDP-02 boundary hypothesis they assume.
8. Evidence contrary to a preferred broad boundary is retained in the dossier and registry.

## 6. Initial gradient

First-pass dossiers:

1. Canidae
2. Bovidae
3. Equidae
4. Ursidae
5. Felidae
6. Muridae
7. Chiroptera
8. Aves test cluster

Canidae is the calibration case. Chiroptera is reserved as a severe broad-boundary test.

## 7. Deliverable gate

PDP-02 first pass is complete only when:

- the family-level candidate inventory is enumerated for scoped terrestrial vertebrates;
- each initial taxon has at least H-BP/H-O/H-F candidates where biologically applicable;
- every candidate has all five evidence dimensions represented or explicitly marked missing;
- every candidate has a classification and downstream burden statement;
- no classification relies on a conventional divergence date as PDP chronology;
- registry/KINDS.md points to the supporting dossier for each assessed candidate.

This framework determines what PDP-03 is allowed to assume. It does not establish that any candidate boundary is historically true.
