# Maturity (boundary)

## The text's requirement

The text requires a male and a female, not a full-grown pair.

## Standing allowance

- Stock may be embarked young, provided it can survive the voyage and breed
  afterward.
- Weaned or fledged juveniles satisfy this for most land-vertebrate plans.
- Latency does not depend on adult size. The genome is the same.

## Why it matters

Juvenile embarkation is the primary belt auxiliary reconciling large-bodied
kinds with hull volume (`2-theory/ark-interface/juvenile-stock.md`,
`2-theory/ark-interface/population-size.md`). It changes logistics, not the
founder genome: a young pair carries the same alleles as an adult pair.

## Limit

Extending dormancy or torpor to kinds that cannot sustain it is a larger
grant than applying it where the kind's biology allows. Registered in
`registry/AUXILIARIES.md` (A-03) and `registry/OPEN-PROBLEMS.md` (OP-05).
