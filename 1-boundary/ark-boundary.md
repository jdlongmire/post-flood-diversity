# Ark Boundary (hull)

Source: Genesis 6:15.

## Dimensions

- 300 by 50 by 30 cubits.
- At a common cubit of about 18 in (45.7 cm): about 450 by 75 by 45 ft
  (137 by 23 by 14 m).
- Three decks: on the order of 100,000 sq ft (about 9,300 m2) of floor;
  volume about 1.5 million cubic feet (about 43,000 m3).
- A cubit of about 20 to 21 in raises this by roughly a third.

## Standing constraints

- Not all volume is animal space. Household, food, water, and waste remain.
- Negative heuristic from the hull: do not embark the species lists. A
  species-level stock is ruled out by volume, independent of genetics.

## Interfaces

- `2-theory/ark-interface/` - population size, juvenile stock, feed, water,
  waste models that must fit this hull.
- `4-models/ark-loading/` - loading models against these dimensions.
- `1-boundary/kind-boundary.md` - kind width determines how many units the
  hull must carry.
