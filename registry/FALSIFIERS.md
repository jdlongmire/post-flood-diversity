# Falsifiers Register

**Status:** Candidate falsifiers noted; formal F-IDs assigned as severe
tests are designed (`5-predictions/severe-tests.md`).

A falsifier states what observation or result would count against the
programme, in advance. Candidates:

- **F-C1** PDP-03 delivers a quantified result that a founder pair's
  four-copy-limited genome cannot encode a whole plan's descendant
  diversity, with the shortfall named and no belt auxiliary closing it
  without revising the stock.
- **F-C2** The Kind Boundary Project (PDP-02) finds that the only viable
  kind width is the species list, which the hull boundary (BC-3) already
  rules out.
- **F-C3** A worked taxon (PDP-06) succeeds only by changing the founder
  population, chronology, or hard-core event: per MC-2 that is a programme
  revision, and the failure to succeed without revision counts against the
  programme as specified.
- **F-C4** Observed diversification rate ceilings (PDP-04) fall short of
  the hardest required unpacking (e.g., Chiroptera under the body-plan
  hypothesis) by an order that no registered auxiliary bridges.

Falsifiers are not predictions of failure; they are the programme's
standing offer of what would change its appraisal.
