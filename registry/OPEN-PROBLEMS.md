# Open Problems

The programme's known deficits, tracked until resolved. New problems get
OP-IDs here before they are cited elsewhere.

| ID | Problem | Owning stream | Status |
|----|---------|---------------|--------|
| OP-01 | **The kind-by-kind account is missing.** This pair, these alleles and pathways, this much selection, these descendants by the catalogues. Until it exists, the frame is a consistent boundary, not an explanation of the diversity. | PDP-06 | open |
| OP-02 | **Founder genome feasibility unquantified.** What a founder pair can encode and transmit under the four-copy constraint is not yet quantified. | PDP-03 | open |
| OP-03 | **Chiroptera as severe test.** Deriving bats from a mammalian founder under the body-plan hypothesis is much harder than deriving dog breeds from a canid-like stock. Success criteria must be pre-registered. | PDP-06 | open |
| OP-04 | **Invertebrate land animals.** Arthropod, snail, worm layouts are a separate list, included only if brought under the same rule. Currently out of scope. | - | deferred |
| OP-05 | **Torpor grant.** Dormancy/torpor where the kind can sustain it is a standing auxiliary; extending it to kinds that cannot is a larger grant needing justification. | PDP-05 | open |
| OP-06 | **Aquatic and insect carriage.** Aquatic animals excluded by the text; insects/groups carried with fodder only if the core's preserved stock is not required for them. Rule needs a clean statement. | PDP-05 | open |
| OP-07 | **Family-level kind enumeration.** The competing hypothesis needs the same evidentiary fields as the body-plan list before PDP-02 can adjudicate. | PDP-02 | open |
