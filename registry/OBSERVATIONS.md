# Observations Register

Recorded observations usable as belt resources (rate examples, empirical
bounds). Per MC-1, they establish biological possibility or rate bounds;
they do not demonstrate the post-Flood history.

## Recorded

| ID | Observation | Use | Filed in |
|----|-------------|-----|----------|
| O-01 | Dogs: wolf-like origin; substantial skull/size diversity over the domestication period, extremes in recent centuries; still one interbreeding species. | Rate bound for within-plan spread; PDP-TAX-001 calibration. | `3-evidence/observed-diversification/dogs.md` |
| O-02 | Livestock (cattle, sheep, cats): breed-level diversity over the historical/archaeological record of domestication, no new body plan. | Rate bound for breed-level unpacking. | `3-evidence/observed-diversification/livestock.md` |
| O-03 | Madeira house mice: several chromosomal races, partial hybrid infertility, in centuries after introduction. | Rate bound for chromosomal differentiation. | `3-evidence/observed-diversification/madeira-mice.md` |
| O-04 | Darwin's finches: beak shifts within decades; one hybrid lineage reproductively isolated in a few generations. | Rate bounds for selection and speciation. | `3-evidence/observed-diversification/darwin-finches.md` |

## Not recorded (not to be smuggled in as observation)

- A timed watch of one pair producing a whole plan's species list.
- A transition from one body plan to another.
- A dated duration taken from fossils or molecular clocks (out of scope
  unless added as an auxiliary).
