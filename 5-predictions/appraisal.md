# Appraisal

**Current appraisal (2026-10-03): PDP-0.**

The framework is a consistent loading-and-unpacking frame: the Flood makes
the origin set small and simultaneous; the Ark carries intact,
heterozygous body-plan stocks, young and small enough for the hull;
catalogues a few thousand years later are unpacked plans, not a second
creation. Rate examples show that within-plan spread from a variable stock
can be rapid. They are not evidence the Flood occurred.

The whole-plan biological grant has not been demonstrated by the observed
examples. Until the kind-by-kind account exists, the frame is a consistent
boundary, not an explanation of the diversity.
