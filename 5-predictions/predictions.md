# Predictions

**Status:** Reserved for PDP-5.

Predictions the programme makes that discriminate it from rival accounts,
registered in advance with the conditions under which they would be
confirmed or disconfirmed.

Nothing is registered yet: at PDP-0 the programme has a boundary
specification, not predictions. Candidate prediction areas (to be
developed):

- Founder-genome signatures: what the four-copy constraint implies for
  patterns of variation within kinds (PDP-03).
- Diversification rate ceilings: what the belt auxiliaries can and cannot
  produce inside the window (PDP-04).
- Kind-boundary markers: where PDP-02 expects discontinuities between
  kinds.

Each prediction gets a P-ID in `registry/PREDICTIONS.md` and traceability
in `traceability/claims.yaml` before it counts.
