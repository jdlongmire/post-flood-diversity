# Population Genetics (evidence stream)

Reserved evidence stream for PDP-03 and PDP-04.

Holds empirical population-genetics findings relevant to founder effects,
heterozygosity retention, and diversification rates: what small or
bottlenecked populations are observed to do, as bounds on what the
programme's founder stocks may invoke.

Charter Rule 1 applies: findings here establish possibility and rate
bounds, not the post-Flood history. Extrapolation to Ark founder
populations is a model claim and belongs in `4-models/`.
