# Research Landscape and Reusable Sources

**Programme:** Post-Flood Diversity Programme (PDP)
**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** literature reconnaissance; source claims require primary-paper validation before formal publication

## 1. Purpose

PDP is not the first creationist programme to investigate created-kind boundaries or rapid post-Flood diversification. This survey identifies predecessor programmes, methods, hypotheses, datasets, and conventional empirical resources that PDP can reuse or retest.

The governing distinction remains Charter Rule 3: observation/measurement, model-dependent inference, auxiliary assumption, and PDP interpretation are not interchangeable.

## 2. Closest predecessor programme: CRS eKINDS

The Creation Research Society eKINDS initiative (Examination of Kinds in Natural Diversification and Speciation) is the closest identified predecessor to PDP.

Published eKINDS work includes:
- founder events as drivers of rapid post-Flood diversification;
- gene-content statistical baraminology;
- Galliformes work establishing baraminic status, evaluating diversity, and reconstructing post-Flood dispersal;
- molecular and anatomical work on ruminant boundaries.

### PDP disposition

**LEVERAGE, DO NOT SIMPLY ADOPT.**

PDP should ingest eKINDS as a predecessor research line and independently register:
- its observations;
- its methods;
- its boundary conclusions;
- its auxiliary hypotheses;
- its unresolved burdens.

Where raw data/code are available, PDP should reproduce the method before relying on the conclusion.

## 3. Direct Canidae predecessor

Jean K. Lightner (2009), "Karyotypic and allelic diversity within the canid baramin (Canidae)," Journal of Creation 23(1):94-98, explicitly proposes:
- Canidae as a single baramin;
- two canids preserved on the Ark;
- a maximum of four alleles per locus in the founder pair;
- present karyotypic and allelic diversity as requiring explanation beyond founder-carried alleles.

This is directly antecedent to CAN-H-F and PDP-03.

### PDP disposition

Register Lightner's Canidae-level boundary as **PREDECESSOR-HYPOTHESIS**, not corroboration. Re-run the burden using modern whole-genome data. Her four-allele observation agrees with PDP's diploid founder constraint, but proposed designed-change explanations remain hypotheses requiring independent appraisal.

## 4. Ark-kind enumeration predecessor

Lightner et al. (2011), "Determining the Ark Kinds," prioritizes hybridization as evidence of reproductive continuity and uses statistical baraminology/cognitum/taxonomy where hybrid data are absent.

Lightner (2012), "Mammalian Ark Kinds," estimated 137 kinds from extant mammals and noted that inclusion of extinct mammalian families could raise the Ark total above 300. The paper treats Canidae as a dog kind and records substantial hybrid evidence.

### PDP disposition

Use the 2011/2012 work as a historical candidate-kind registry and evidence index. Do not freeze its 2012 taxonomy. Crosswalk every candidate against MDD v2.5 and current genomic/hybrid evidence.

## 5. Boundary-analysis methods worth reproducing

### 5.1 Hybridization continuity
Strength: direct evidence that reproductive barriers are incomplete.
Limit: absence of a known hybrid is weak negative evidence; historical compatibility may not equal extant compatibility.

### 5.2 Baraminic Distance Correlation / statistical baraminology
Todd Wood's review traces BDC to Robinson/Cavanaugh and notes character-selection sensitivity. It can be reproduced as a morphology/character clustering tool, but raw distance is not itself a kind identifier.

### 5.3 Gene Content Method (GCM)
O'Micks (2017) compares common orthologous gene content using Jaccard similarity and clustering; it also introduces pan-genome/completeness metrics under a creationist gene-loss interpretation.

PDP use: reproduce the similarity/clustering measurements. Treat claims about archebaramin erosion as AUX/PDP rather than OBS.

### 5.4 Whole Genome K-mer Signature (WGKS)
Cserhati developed a whole-genome k-mer comparison method and compared it against mitochondrial and gene-content clustering. Later eKINDS ruminant work used GCM plus WGKS.

PDP use: potentially valuable because it avoids dependence on a single gene tree. However, k-mer similarity does not by itself identify historical kind boundaries. Benchmark it against known within-population, within-family, and between-family comparisons.

### 5.5 Multi-evidence heuristic
Ahlquist and Lightner's Galliformes series combines biblical constraints, hybridization, morphology, molecular data, diversity characterization, and dispersal reconstruction.

PDP use: strong architectural predecessor for PDP-02/PDP-06, with PDP's stricter observation/inference/auxiliary tagging added.

## 6. Post-Flood diversification hypotheses to register as predecessors

These are candidate ancillary mechanisms, not established PDP mechanisms.

### PH-01 Founder-event sorting
Lightner & Ahlquist (2017): repeated founder events after the Flood can sort ancestral alleles and accelerate population differentiation.

PDP disposition: HIGH PRIORITY TO TEST. Founder effects are an established population-genetic phenomenon; the specifically post-Flood historical application is PDP/AUX.

### PH-02 AGE hypothesis
Todd Wood (2002): "Altruistic Genetic Elements," mobile/transposable elements proposed to drive rapid intrabaraminic diversification.

PDP disposition: HISTORIC PREDECESSOR AH. Translate into modern transposable-element/regulatory-genomics terms and test specific predictions rather than inherit the teleological label.

### PH-03 VIGE/baranome hypothesis
Peer Terborg: pluripotent "baranomes" plus variation-inducing genetic elements such as transposons, ERVs, LINEs, SINEs, microsatellites and regulatory mechanisms.

PDP disposition: HYPOTHESIS FAMILY. Decompose into individually testable mechanisms. "Baranome" is a model construct, not an observed genomic object.

### PH-04 Hemizygosity / supergene latent-trait hypothesis
Terborg proposes originally hemizygous feature modules as a way to store latent trait potential that can be fixed/lost through population processes.

PDP disposition: TESTABLE IN PART. Search extant taxa for supergene/hemizygous architectures and quantify whether such architectures materially relax founder-burden constraints. Do not generalize from examples without a genome-wide model.

### PH-05 Created heterozygosity
Common creationist proposal that founders carried high designed standing heterozygosity.

PDP disposition: BASELINE AUXILIARY ALREADY PRESENT AS A-02a. PDP-03 must quantify its ceiling rather than invoke it qualitatively.

## 7. Conventional empirical resources PDP should leverage

Use these primarily for OBS and method layers.

### Taxonomy
- American Society of Mammalogists Mammal Diversity Database v2.5 (2026): 6,904 species, 27 orders, 169 families. Versioned and downloadable.

### Canidae genomics
- PRJNA822671: South American canid whole-genome reads; 18 BioSamples, 27 SRA experiments, 1,514 Gbases; maned wolf and bush dog assemblies.
- de Chavez et al. (2022), PNAS: South American canid comparative genomics; raw data and public scripts.
- PRJNA494815: eight Caninae species.
- PRJNA494719: 28 Canis individuals.
- Dog10K phase one / PRJNA648123 and PRJEB62420: large canid variant resource.
- UU_Cfam_GSD_1.0 / GCF_011100685.1: reference coordinate system used in Dog10K phase one.

### Hybridization / reticulation
Modern whole-genome Canis studies provide measured sequence data and statistical evidence of admixture/gene flow. PDP may reuse the observations/statistics while separately classifying the historical reconstruction.

## 8. Programme gap PDP can fill

Existing creationist work is strongest at:
- proposed kind delineation;
- hybridization continuity;
- qualitative/cluster-based genomics;
- founder-effect arguments;
- proposed designed-diversification mechanisms.

The identified gap is the one PDP-03 is designed to attack:

> Given a specifically defined founder stock and modern whole-genome observations, what is the quantitative minimum genomic burden that cannot have been carried as standing allelic variation, and can specified post-founder mechanisms cover that burden inside the programme chronology?

This distinguishes PDP from simply adding another baraminology clustering method.
