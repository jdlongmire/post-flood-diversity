# Madeira Mice

**Status:** Recorded observation. Usable as a rate example (charter Rule 1).

## What is recorded

- House mice introduced to Madeira by humans.
- Several chromosomal races in the centuries after introduction.
- Partial hybrid infertility between races.

## What it establishes

That chromosomal differentiation and partial reproductive isolation can arise
in centuries from an introduced stock: an empirical rate bound for the
chromosomal-change auxiliary (`2-theory/genetics/chromosomal-change.md`).

## What it does not establish

That the post-Flood history occurred (charter Rule 1).
