# Dogs

**Status:** Recorded observation. Usable as a rate example (charter Rule 1).

## What is recorded

- Wolf-like origin.
- Substantial skull and size diversity over the domestication period,
  extremes concentrated in recent centuries of breeding.
- Still one interbreeding species.

## What it establishes

That substantial morphological diversity can arise within a plan from a
variable stock without requiring a new body plan. It sets an empirical rate
bound for within-plan spread and gives PDP-TAX-001 (Canidae) its calibration
case.

## What it does not establish

- That a single founder pair produced the whole canid species list.
- That the post-Flood history occurred.

Per charter Rule 1: biological possibility and rate bounds, yes;
demonstration of the history, no. The seed specification is explicit that the
watched cases do not demonstrate the whole-plan grant for one pair.
