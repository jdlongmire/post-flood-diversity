# Livestock

**Status:** Recorded observation. Usable as a rate example (charter Rule 1).

## What is recorded

- Cattle, sheep, cats: breed-level diversity over the historical and
  archaeological record of domestication.
- Achieved without a new body plan.

## What it establishes

That breed-level diversity accumulates within plans on historical
timescales from managed variable stocks: another empirical rate bound for
within-plan unpacking.

## What it does not establish

That the post-Flood history occurred, or that unmanaged founder pairs
produce equivalent diversity on the programme's timeline. Rate bounds, not
demonstrations (charter Rule 1).
