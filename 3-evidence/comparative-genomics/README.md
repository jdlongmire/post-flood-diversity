# Comparative Genomics (evidence stream)

Reserved evidence stream for PDP-02 and PDP-03.

Holds comparative-genomic findings relevant to kind boundaries
(phylogenetic structure, genomic architecture across candidate kinds) and
to founder feasibility (e.g., regulatory differences underlying breed or
ecotype differences).

Charter Rule 1 applies: findings here bound what mechanisms may be
invoked; they do not demonstrate the post-Flood history.
