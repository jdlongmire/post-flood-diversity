# Juvenile Stock

Young, small representatives of each kind, embarked under the maturity
boundary (`1-boundary/maturity.md`).

## Standing allowance

The text requires a male and a female, not a full-grown pair. Weaned or
fledged juveniles satisfy this for most land-vertebrate plans, provided they
can survive the voyage and breed afterward. Latency does not depend on adult
size: the genome is the same.

## Role in the programme

The primary belt auxiliary reconciling large-bodied kinds with hull volume.
It changes logistics, not the founder genome: a young pair carries the same
alleles as an adult pair, so the allele cap
(`../genetics/allele-constraints.md`) is unaffected.
