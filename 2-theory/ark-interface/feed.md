# Feed

Hull logistics auxiliary: feeding the embarked stock for the voyage.

## Standing auxiliaries

- Dense shared feed rather than exclusive fresh diets.
- Fodder interval counted at maintenance, not full growth, for the voyage
  duration actually used.
- Dormancy or torpor where the kind can sustain it. Extending torpor to kinds
  that cannot is a larger grant (registered: `registry/AUXILIARIES.md` A-03,
  `registry/OPEN-PROBLEMS.md` OP-05).

## Role in the programme

Feed is a boundary logistics problem, not a genetic one. It constrains which
kind-width and maturity assumptions are jointly viable with the hull, and is
modeled in `4-models/ark-loading/` under PDP-05.

## Status

Concept stated; quantified provisioning models reserved for PDP-05.
