# Speciation

Speciation, including reproductive isolation where applicable: the endpoint
of the PDP-06 pipeline for lineages that diverge to species level.

## Rate references

- Darwin's finches: one hybrid lineage reproductively isolated from local
  birds in a few generations
  (`3-evidence/observed-diversification/darwin-finches.md`).
- Madeira house mice: several chromosomal races with partial hybrid
  infertility in centuries
  (`3-evidence/observed-diversification/madeira-mice.md`).

Both establish that reproductive isolation can arise fast; neither
demonstrates the post-Flood history (charter Rule 1).

## Role in the programme

The pipeline step after population differentiation: isolation and dispersal
produce differentiated populations; speciation mechanisms (including
chromosomal change) produce reproductive isolation where applicable; the
result is the observed descendant diversity at the catalogues.

## Status

Mechanism stated; worked application reserved for PDP-06 taxa
(`4-models/taxa/`).
