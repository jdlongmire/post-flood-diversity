# Isolation

Isolation as the animals spread: geographic and ecological separation that
lets differentiated populations diverge.

## Role in the programme

- Follows dispersal (`../ecology/dispersal.md`): as founder descendants
  spread across post-Flood landscapes, isolation partitions variation.
- Precedes and enables speciation (`speciation.md`) and chromosomal
  differentiation (`../genetics/chromosomal-change.md`).
