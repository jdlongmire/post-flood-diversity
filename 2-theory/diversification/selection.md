# Selection

Selection in emptied habitats: the ecological engine of the diversification
auxiliaries.

## Role in the programme

- Post-Flood landscapes present emptied niches (`../ecology/empty-niches.md`).
  Selection acts on the founder stock's standing variation (and later
  mutation) as populations expand into them.
- Works with recombination (`../genetics/recombination.md`) and regulatory
  variation (`../genetics/regulatory-variation.md`) to convert variation into
  differentiation.

## Rate reference

Darwin's finches: beak size and shape shifts within decades
(`3-evidence/observed-diversification/darwin-finches.md`). Establishes that
selection can move morphology fast given variable stock; does not
demonstrate the post-Flood history (charter Rule 1).
