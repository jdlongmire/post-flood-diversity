# Mutation

Mutation added inside the time boundary: the only source of genuinely new
alleles after the bottleneck.

## Role in the programme

- Descendants have the founders' alleles plus mutations that arise and
  spread later (`allele-constraints.md`).
- Operates within the unpacking window (`1-boundary/chronology.md`):
  about 5,500 years to the mid-1700s catalogues.

## Constraints

- Mutation must do its work inside the stipulated window; rate assumptions
  are belt auxiliaries and must be registered (`registry/AUXILIARIES.md`).
- Invoking mutation rates or spectra from outside the programme's boundary
  (e.g., deep-time calibrations) requires explicit auxiliary registration;
  dated durations from fossils or molecular clocks are otherwise out of
  scope (`0-programme/scope.md`).
