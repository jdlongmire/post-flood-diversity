# Standing Variation

The primary genetic auxiliary: later within-plan variety is largely an
unpacking of heterozygous variation the founder stock already carried.

## The route

- The preserved stock is treated as intact and heterozygous (hard-core
  commitment 3), not as an impoverished remnant.
- Polygenic recombination of alleles the pair already carried generates
  descendant diversity without requiring a distinct founder allele for every
  later breed or species. The seed specification explicitly drops that
  necessity.
- Standing variation plus post-Flood mutation is the input to recombination
  and selection in the PDP-06 pipeline (`0-programme/charter.md`).

## What stays inside the records

This auxiliary is consistent with the observed rate examples
(`3-evidence/observed-diversification/`): substantial within-plan spread from
a variable stock can be rapid. Per charter Rule 1, those observations
establish possibility and rate bounds; they do not demonstrate the post-Flood
history.

## Open quantification

How much heterozygous variation a single pair can carry, and whether it
suffices for a whole plan's species list, is the open question PDP-03 must
quantify (`2-theory/genetics/allele-constraints.md`,
`registry/OPEN-PROBLEMS.md` OP-02).
