# Regulatory Variation

Regulatory variation as a belt auxiliary: changes in when, where, and how
much a gene is expressed, rather than changes to protein-coding sequence.

## Role in the programme

- Offers a route to substantial phenotypic change from a limited founder
  allele set, since regulatory differences can rewire development without
  new coding alleles.
- Works alongside polygenic recombination (`recombination.md`) and selection
  (`../diversification/selection.md`) in the PDP-06 pipeline.

## Status

Mechanism stated; quantification reserved for PDP-03/PDP-04. Comparative
evidence (e.g., regulatory differences underlying breed or ecotype
differences in observed-diversification cases) belongs in
`3-evidence/comparative-genomics/` when gathered.
