# Chromosomal Change

Chromosomal rearrangement as a belt auxiliary: fusions, fissions, and
rearrangements that can accompany and reinforce population differentiation.

## Rate reference

Madeira house mice: several chromosomal races, with partial hybrid
infertility, in the centuries after human introduction
(`3-evidence/observed-diversification/madeira-mice.md`). Usable as an
empirical rate bound for how fast chromosomal differentiation can proceed;
not a demonstration of the post-Flood history (charter Rule 1).

## Role in the programme

- A route from population differentiation to reproductive isolation where
  applicable, in the PDP-06 pipeline.
- Interacts with the isolation auxiliary
  (`2-theory/diversification/isolation.md`).

## Status

Mechanism stated; case-specific application reserved for PDP-06 worked
taxa (`4-models/taxa/`).
