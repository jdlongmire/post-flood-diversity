# Recombination

Polygenic recombination of alleles the founder pair already carried.

## What recombination does

- Reshuffles the standing variation of the founder stock into descendant
  combinations, producing morphological and physiological diversity without
  new alleles.
- Stays inside the records: it is one of the belt auxiliaries consistent
  with the observed rate examples.

## What recombination does not do

- It does not invent a fifth allele at a locus (`allele-constraints.md`).
  Recombination rearranges; mutation adds.
- It does not, by itself, demonstrate the post-Flood history (charter
  Rule 1).
