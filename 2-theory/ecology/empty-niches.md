# Empty Niches

Post-Flood landscapes present emptied niches: habitats vacant of the
pre-Flood fauna, available for founder descendants to expand into.

## Role in the programme

- Provides the ecological opportunity that makes selection
  (`../diversification/selection.md`) effective: variation meets vacancy.
- Part of the PDP-06 pipeline between recombination/selection and
  isolation/dispersal.

## Status

Concept stated. Ecological modeling reserved for PDP-04/PDP-06; dispersal
routes in `dispersal.md`, biogeographic patterns in `biogeography.md`.
