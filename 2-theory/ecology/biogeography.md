# Biogeography

The distribution of descendant diversity across post-Flood landscapes:
which lineages ended up where, and whether the programme's dispersal and
isolation mechanisms can account for it.

## Role in the programme

- A downstream check on PDP-06: worked kinds must not only produce the
  right diversity but place it plausibly.
- Fed by `dispersal.md` and `../diversification/isolation.md`.

## Evidence home

`3-evidence/biogeography/` holds the empirical patterns; this file holds
the theoretical account. They are kept separate so patterns are not
confused with explanations (charter Rule 1).
