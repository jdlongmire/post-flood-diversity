# Dispersal

Dispersal of founder descendants from the Ark landing region across
post-Flood landscapes.

## Role in the programme

- Moves populations into emptied niches (`empty-niches.md`), where
  selection and isolation (`../diversification/isolation.md`) operate.
- The mechanism behind the biogeographic patterns the programme must
  eventually account for (`biogeography.md`).

## Boundary

Dispersal is a biological/ecological claim inside PDP. It is interfaced
with, not conflated with, Flood geology: the geological mechanism of
landscape formation belongs upstream
(`jdlongmire/global-flood-hydrotectonic-model`).

## Status

Concept stated; routes and rates reserved for PDP-05/PDP-06.
