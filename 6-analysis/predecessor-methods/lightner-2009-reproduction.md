# Lightner 2009 Canidae Reproduction/Update Protocol

## Original claim set

Lightner (2009) proposes Canidae as a single baramin descended from one Ark pair and explicitly notes:
- at most four founder alleles at a gene/locus;
- substantial present allelic diversity beyond that ceiling at examined loci;
- extensive canid karyotype diversity;
- a need for mechanisms beyond founder-carried diversity alone.

PDP classification:
- Ark pair and Canidae boundary: PREDECESSOR AUX/HYPOTHESIS.
- Four physical copies at an autosomal locus in a diploid pair: constraint.
- Reported karyotypes/alleles: OBS subject to source validation.
- Designed/directed-change interpretation: AUX-HYPOTHESIS, not OBS.

## Update tasks

1. Rebuild the canid species/genus list against current taxonomy.
2. Replace 2009 karyotype coverage with current chromosome/assembly evidence.
3. Replace selected protein-coding allele examples with whole-genome locus distributions.
4. Distinguish nucleotide states, haplotypes, structural variants, CNVs, and chromosome rearrangements.
5. Re-evaluate whether the original explanatory deficit persists, grows, or changes category.
6. Do not assume Lightner's proposed mechanism from the existence of the deficit.

## Relationship to PDP-03-001

Lightner identified the burden qualitatively. PDP-03-001 attempts to quantify a defensible portion of it using explicit homologous-locus definitions and modern genomic data.
