## Pre-registered interpretation

Agreement among GCM and WGKS that Canidae forms a coherent cluster would strengthen the case that CAN-H-F is a biologically meaningful candidate partition, but it would not demonstrate Ark-kind identity.

Disagreement is also informative. It must be traced to the measurement layer, data quality, taxon sampling, or method assumptions rather than resolved by choosing the method that favors PDP.

PDP-03-001 remains logically distinct: it asks what genomic burden follows **if** CAN-H-F is assumed.
